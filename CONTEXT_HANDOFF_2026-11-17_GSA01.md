## What ran

3 models × 9 prompts × (1 greedy + 5 sampling strategies × 3 seeds) = 432 generation cases, 128 bytes each. Device: `cuda`; PyTorch: `2.13.0+rocm7.1`. All three checkpoints loaded strictly, greedy parity passed for all models, and the latent factor cache passed bit-identical cached/uncached logits validation. The audit did not train or modify checkpoints. A separate test-run log is not stored, so unit-test execution is not independently verified.

## Key result

Mean repeated-trigram rates:

| Model | Greedy | T=0.7 | T=0.9 | T=1.0 | Top-p .90 | Top-p .95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dense | 0.392 | 0.253 | 0.235 | 0.200 | 0.254 | 0.237 |
| Low-rank | 0.882 | 0.465 | 0.278 | 0.214 | 0.388 | 0.247 |
| Latent | 0.862 | 0.387 | 0.302 | 0.186 | 0.313 | 0.277 |

Sampling materially reduces greedy repetition, especially for low-rank and latent. At temperature 1.0, unique-byte fractions are 0.271/0.261/0.261 (dense/low-rank/latent); distinct-bigram fractions are 0.669/0.653/0.678. UTF-8 validity is 24/27, 24/27, and 25/27 respectively. Top-p configurations improve UTF-8 validity in most/all cases but do not establish better language.

## Qualitative interpretation

All four held-out prompts fail to elicit faithful continuations from all three models. Greedy low-rank and latent outputs often collapse into repeated fragments or whitespace. Sampling breaks up those loops, but the resulting text is mostly malformed code-like mixtures rather than coherent continuation. Dense is less collapsed, but also frequently off-target.

**Supported:** Greedy argmax decoding materially amplifies repetition in low-rank and latent checkpoints.

**Not supported:** That useful language capability is merely hidden by greedy decoding, or that the latent representation has language quality comparable to dense. More diversity and UTF-8 validity are not quality measures.

## Caveats

- Only five fixed prompts and four held-out excerpts; 128 generated bytes; three sampling seeds.
- Source-code-heavy, mixed-domain corpus; not a general language benchmark.
- Held-out excerpts come from the final 10% of the same corpus, not an external benchmark.
- Raw entropy is averaged along each strategy's own generated trajectory; strategy comparisons therefore include context drift and are not fixed-context distribution comparisons.
- Aggregate statistics are descriptive, not significance tests.

## Research decision / next step

Close GSA-01 as a decoding diagnostic. Do not run another temperature sweep. The next experiment should discriminate between (a) corpus/domain coherence and next-byte distribution quality and (b) limitations introduced by the latent/shared-constructor representation. First define fixed-context continuation evaluation and explicit continuation-quality criteria, then compare the existing dense, low-rank, and latent checkpoints under matched contexts. Propose one narrow experiment before implementation.
