## 2026-11-27 — Establish one project identity and operating system

- **Decision:** Use **Northstar** as the single conversational codename for the active research effort.
- **Reason:** Experiment labels and version names were causing avoidable confusion in collaboration.
- **Project direction:** Prioritize the shared-constructor language-model line as the current lead, subject to an artifact audit. This does not declare it successful or erase other research branches.
- **File policy:** Preserve historical experiment folders and files. Add a canonical Northstar index and ledger rather than performing a risky mass rename or move.
- **Process policy:** Require an experiment contract before substantive implementation; disclose scope changes before making them; distinguish inspected, written, tested, run, measured, estimated, and inferred.
- **User-facing policy:** Refer to the work as Northstar. The assistant tracks internal filenames and experiment IDs and asks when ambiguous instead of guessing.
